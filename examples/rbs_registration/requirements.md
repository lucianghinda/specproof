# Registration eligibility rule

An applicant is eligible exactly when they are at least 18 years old and have
given consent. Applicants younger than 18 are ineligible. Missing consent and
explicitly false consent both mean the applicant has not given consent and are
ineligible. An 18-year-old who consents is eligible.

This is the expected-result rule for the example tests. The RBS signature only
describes accepted input types; it does not provide this behavior rule.
